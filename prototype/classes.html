Family node grammar — each family decides a different kind of trade

A shotgun's branch is slug vs scatter; an MG's is sustain vs burst; a rifle's is marksman vs duelist. Eleven families that all read as "more damage vs more range" is one family with a reskin on.

The concept

"Differences in abilities of game entities that are in type rather than in value."
Orthogonal Differentiation — GDP3 Game Design Pattern Library, Chalmers University of Technology / University of Gothenburg. Coined by Harvey Smith (GDC 2003), formalised by Björk & Holopainen (2004).
gdp.cse.chalmers.se · verified 2026-11-07
The original already had the backbone: 7 of its 9 weapon families were class-locked. The flaw was where the boundary was drawn — the Soldier held 6 of 9 families and 97 of 185 weapons; Gunner and Commando held 2 each. One class was a superset of the game.

Art direction — the Engineer

Render rules (read from the original's own art): top-heavy heroic proportion, not chibi — ~8 heads tall, small head, massive shoulders. Angular near-square jaw, minimal chin. Exaggerated hands and boots (~1.5×) to sell action. Thick variable-width black ink outline, thinner interior detail lines. Cel-shading with soft digital-paint shadows, subtle canvas texture. Sharp white rim highlights on leather. Weapons held away from the body for silhouette readability — never shouldered like a real rifle.

The Engineer reads as a tradesman: satchel, tool roll, mechanic's bib, goggles pushed up on the forehead, and a bolted-on mechanical silhouette so the class is unmistakable as a 32 px minimap dot. Industrial yellow-ochre — deliberately outside the Royals' blue-grey and Nationals' drab olive, because the Engineer serves whichever faction holds the field.
