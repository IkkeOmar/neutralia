Vehicle Variant Precedent — research

All sources fetched live 2026-11-08. Claims marked [verified] are traceable to a source actually retrieved. Where a source could not be fetched, that is stated rather than filled in from memory.

Purpose: inform systems/vehicle-system.md — Omar asked whether BFH's small vehicle roster could grow with "different specialized types for the different ones there are already".

Faction variants: Willys Jeep / Kübelwagen · M4 Sherman / Panzer IV · Spitfire / Messerschmitt. "Aside from emplacements and naval craft, all vehicles are faction specific and have slight differences from one another. Each faction has access to four vehicles: jeeps, medium tanks, fighter planes, and helicopters." [verified]

Seat mechanics: - Jeep: "Transports up to three players with one driver and two passengers, passengers can fire their own weapons (including snipers)." [verified] - Light tank: "Fires a powerful mounted cannon and can transport one passenger in the machine gun turret on top of the tank and one driver. The passenger can fire their own weapons, and hide within the tank by ducking." [verified] - Plane: "The only air unit so far, transports up to two passengers sitting on the wings of the plane and one driver. [verified] - AA gun: "One man operated immobile turret, does heavy damage to planes and weak damage to anything else." [verified]

Map gating (measured): parsed from bfh-info.cs-heroes.cz/en/game/map/ → research/bfh_map_vehicles.json. 5 of 14 maps have zero vehicles (Riverside Rush, Midnight Mayhem, Perilous Port, Lunar Landing, Morning Mayhem). Jeep appears on 6 maps; every other chassis on at most 5. [verified]

The gap: BFH had six chassis and no variants. Every vehicle of a role was the same vehicle. No choice at the spawn screen beyond "wheels or not".

B. BF1 — the direct precedent (chassis × role-variant)

[verified] — gameranx.com/features/id/52014/article/battlefield-1-vehicles-2016/, battlefield1-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicle, gamespot.com/gallery/all-of-battlefield-1s-vehicles/2900-940/.

BF1 shipped four chassis and three variants each — twelve vehicles — and each variant was a different vehicle in practice:

Chassis Variants
FT-17 Light Tank Close Support (canister shell, poison gas, deployable medkit) · Flanker (20mm auto-cannon, anti-tank mines, quick mobility restore) · Howitzer (75mm, smokescreen, emergency repair)
A7V Heavy Tank Heavy Assault (57mm + 8mm MG 08 ×2 + 4 MG seats, track repair, smokescreen) · Breakthrough (57mm + 20mm auto-cannon, medical crate, emergency repair) · Heavy Flamethrower (gas emitter, track repair, flamethrowers)
Mark V Landship Squad Support (1 driver + 4 gunners, hotchkiss cannon + 6-pounders, track repair + ammo + medical crates) · Tank Hunter (13.2mm anti-tank cannon + 6-pounders, gas emitter, emergency repair) · Mortar (rear mortar firing smoke/gas shells)
Artillery Truck Armored Mortar Truck · Armored Anti-Aircraft Truck · Armored Artillery Truck

Two properties we copy:

  1. Variants change the seat count, not only the gun. Landship Squad Support carries five; Tank Hunter carries three. Heavy Assault carries six; Breakthrough carries four. [verified] — "the A7V can carry up to six players at once depending on the variant you select."
  2. Variants are gated by package, and the package is the chassis. In BF1 you owned a class (tank / truck / landship) and unlocked its variants by playing. That maps onto our chassis → variant unlock model exactly.

Naming precedent (BF1 role words): Tank Hunter · Squad Support · Close Support · Flanker · Howitzer · Breakthrough · Heavy Assault · Heavy Flamethrower · Mortar. These are function words, not flavour words — they say what the vehicle does.

C. BF2042 — the cautionary tale with named failure modes

[verified] — ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-2042/news/dev-notes-state-of-vehicle-gameplay (EA dev notes, 30 Nov 2023). This is a primary developer source and it is unusually candid. Direct quotes:

"Frequent feedback we've heard from the Community was around the prevalence of explosive weaponry, and unclear combat roles across vehicles."

"During Season 5, we fully overhauled all vehicle loadouts to ensure combat roles were once again clearly defined. This largely meant that some vehicles such as the LATV4 Recon which was meant as a transport vehicle, no longer had access to explosive weaponry."

"The team is also happy with how the changes to the EMKV90-TOR have been received by the community, and how it moved away from being a notorious vehicle for sniping from afar. We felt reducing its long-range combat potency was necessary, but to compensate, we also increased its close combat power to allow for a more aggressive role."

"In previous Battlefield titles, jets could dominate an entire match by themselves. We didn't want that for Battlefield 2042 and changed their role to focus on air combat, while making ground support capabilities secondary."

"Our current intent for the stealth helicopters is to be a more casual gameplay option versus their attack helicopter counterparts, which offer higher risk vs reward type gameplay."

Lessons encoded in systems/vehicle-system.md as rules R1–R3: transport carries no explosives · each variant owns one battlefield line · air does not ground-clear.

D. BF2 — chassis as role separation (the MMO-scale contrast)

[verified] — gamespot.com/articles/battlefield-2-walkthrough/1100-6127838/.

BF2 gave each side "a main battle tank, an anti-aircraft vehicle, and so on", and the GameSpot walkthrough describes them by role rather than by chassis:

  • Main Battle Tank (M1A2 / T-90 / Type 98): kills most land vehicles in 3–4 hits, one-shots small vehicles, long reload, arc with shell drop. One passenger turret machinegun seat that can duck.
  • Anti-aircraft vehicle: separate chassis with a dedicated role.
  • APC: "most APCs will require only a couple of hits to be destroyed".
  • FAAV / HMMWV: "blown away in a single shot."

Contrast with WoT: BF2 has role-per-chassis — the tank is not a tank variant of something else. BF1 has role-per-variant on a shared chassis. WoT has role-per-class within a chassis as well, which is the MMO contrast we explicitly do not want (see §F).

Interesting detail: in BF2 the tank gunner's seat is exposed but duckable — the same affordance as the BFH turret gunner. Risk is opt-in, not forced.

E. WoT / War Thunder — the class-tree contrast

[verified] — worldoftanks.com/en/content/guide/game-mechanics-and-achievements/vehicle-types-and-functions/.

World of Tanks has five vehicle types with a defined battlefield role each:

Type Definition (verbatim from the guide)
Light tank "Maneuverable vehicles with compact dimensions and good concealment and view range, but with poor armoring and firepower."
Medium tank "Versatile units, quite maneuverable, with considerable firepower and survivability."
Heavy tank "low maneuverability in general, but decent armor and powerful guns."
Tank destroyer "weakly armored vehicles with very powerful and accurate guns with great armor penetration… can be ineffective and vulnerable when fighting on their own."
SPG "Slow and poorly armored assault guns with high-explosive shells firing at long distances can stun or cause damage to several vehicles at once."

WoT also splits role from type (from Tier VI): Assault, Versatile, Breakthrough, Sniper, Support, Scout. Each role states its battlefield line — Assault holds the front, Scout spots, Sniper destroys from cover, Support relies on coordination.

WoT additionally splits the map into three battle lines: contact line (heavy armour pushes, light tanks spot), support line, and rear line (snipers and artillery). [verified]

Why this is a contrast, not a model. WoT's tree is a 10-tier progression per nation with crew skills, equipment and research. It is excellent for a dedicated tank game and wrong for NEUTRALIA: it requires a long horizontal grind before a player has any choice, and it makes vehicle progression its own game rather than a layer of the shooter. Our BF1-derived model gives role choice immediately and gates variants by playing the chassis, which is the same trick as the per-weapon skill tree.

The battle-line vocabulary is worth borrowing, though: "assault / breakthrough / support / scout / sniper" are exactly the role words BF1 used and exactly the words our variant names use.

War Thunder adds battle rating per vehicle, used for matchmaking — [verified] — wiki.warthunder.com/mechanics/matchmaking, metammo.com/war-thunder-guide/ranks-and-br. This is the precedent closest to Omar's "honor" idea: a rating that decides who you fight, separate from the vehicle's own power.

F. Naming conventions that read in a HUD

[verified] across BF1, BF2, BF3/2042 and WoT.

Convention Example Where
Role word as the name Tank Hunter, Squad Support, Flanker, Howitzer BF1
Chassis suffix LAV-AD, HMMWV TOW, M1045 "TOW Carrier", Flakpanzer IV, Valentine AA Mk I, Churchill Gun Carrier, StuG III, M7 Priest BF2 / BF3
Class abbreviation MBT, IFV, AA, SPG, APC, FAV universal — instant HUD readability
Mark numbering Mk VA/VB, G-2/G-6, A1/A2, Ausf.E/F BF1 / WoT
Tier roman numerals "I–VIII" WoT / War Thunder
Flavour prefix "Super" (Super Hind, Super Hokum) BF2042
Improvised category "Technical" (NSTV, light improvised fighting vehicle) en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_(vehicle)
Aircraft role split Fighter vs Fighter-Bomber (IL-2, SBD, Val, Stuka) BF1942

Our choice: role words for variants (Scout, Courier, Javelin, Hunter, Dug-in, Interceptor, Bomber, Spotter), chassis class abbreviations for the HUD (SC scout car, AC armoured car, LT, HT, FIG, HEL, BOAT). Role words are what BF1 proved works — they say what the vehicle does, and the player reads the spawn screen in half a second.

G. Failure modes to avoid

G1. Unclear combat roles (BF2042's named problem)

[verified] — EA dev notes, quoted in §C. The symptom is "explosive weaponry everywhere" and "unclear combat roles across vehicles". The cause is variants that are all-purpose.

Avoid: any variant that is a good all-rounder. Every variant needs one line it owns.

G2. Vehicles that dominate the match (BF2042's jets)

[verified] — "In previous Battlefield titles, jets could dominate an entire match by themselves." Fixed by making the jet's role air-primary and ground-secondary.

Avoid: an air or artillery chassis that can clear infantry at range while being safe from return fire.

G3. The artillery truck (spawn camping)

[verified] — community complaint threads surfaced in research (reddit.com/r/battlefield_one/comments/1bdf53p/, .../6279xl/): mortar/artillery trucks posted from spawn, accumulating kills with zero exposure.

Avoid: an indirect-fire variant that can shell a spawn point from safety. Mitigations in our design: the Siege tank has a 90° traverse limit in BF1 terms, the Boat is water- bound, and the Spotter helicopter has no weapons at all.

G4. Tank-behind-tank (BF2 style, not a failure per se but a scale warning)

[verified] — BF2's GameSpot walkthrough: a tank takes "three or four" hits from another tank. BFH's tank took 180 HP with much smaller maps and 5-minute rounds.

Avoid: designing vehicle-vs-vehicle exchanges around HP pools at a scale where one tank is worth 50 infantry. NEUTRALIA's maps are large; a vehicle that can be killed by one infantry AT weapon keeps infantry relevant.

G5. Variants that are pure stat reskins

Avoid: a "plasma tank" that is the same tank with +10% damage. Orthogonal Differentiation (the pattern already used in systems/class-and-weapon-families.md) requires the difference to be in type rather than in degree. A variant must change what the vehicle can do.

G2. Five more Battlefield chassis→variant structures (added 2026-11-08)

A parallel research pass covered BF1942, BF2142, BF3, BF4 and BFV. These are the alternative readings of the same problem, and they are the reason our model is BF1's rather than someone else's.

Game Chassis → variant structure What it teaches
BF1942 Chassis is the tier: Medium tank (M4 Sherman / Panzer IV / T-34-85), Heavy tank (M10 Wolverine / Tiger I), SPG (M7 Priest / Wespe), APC (M3 Half-track / SdKfz 251), Jeep (Willys MB / Kübelwagen). Aircraft subdivided by role: Fighter vs Fighter-Bomber vs Medium-Bomber vs Heavy-Bomber. The earliest example of role carried by aircraft subtype, not by cosmetics.
BF2 Six mirrored land classes: MBT, IFV/APC, AA, Transport (jeep), FAV, Attack Heli. Faction counterparts are "almost purely aesthetic" for MBTs — but the three IFVs differ functionally (BTR-90 2A42 = higher ROF; LAV-25 = harder to hit + 2 rear gun ports). Role-per-chassis. Also: a "cosmetic" faction pair can still differ functionally if you look at the secondary systems.
BF2142 Six chassis × two factions: Battlewalker, Tank (A8 Tiger vs hover Type 21 Nekomata), APC, Gunship, Air Transport, FAV. The cautionary read. Tiger vs Nekomata are the same slot but asymmetric by mobility — a deliberate split that players found confusing. We avoid asymmetry within a chassis: every NEUTRALIA variant differs by role, not by one handling quirk.
BF3 24 vehicles per faction. First to layer specializations on chassis (Reactive Armor, Thermal Optics, IR Smoke). Real chassis-reuse naming: LAV-AD = LAV-25 hull + Avenger AA system. Chassis reuse already existed; what BF3 lacked was a readable split. Its jets were "practically the same class, with few differences" — DICE's own words.
BF4 Fixed BF3's jet sameness: Stealth Jet (F-35 / Su-50 / J-20) vs Attack Jet (A-10 / Su-25TM / Q-5 Fantan), separate unlock trees. The direct precedent for rule R9. DICE: in BF3 the two jet classes "were both practically the same class, with few differences".
BFV One chassis + a 6-tier specialization tree (Panzer IV, Tiger I, Staghound). Chassis-reuse suffixes: Flakpanzer IV, Valentine AA Mk I, Churchill Gun Carrier, StuG III. The alternative model: vertical specialisation of one hull rather than horizontal variants of several. We chose horizontal — see §F.

Rule R9 is BF3 → BF4. DICE, in the BF4 vehicles Battlelog: "in Battlefield 3, Fighter Jets and Attack Jets were fairly homogenous and shared the same unlocks. They were both practically the same class, with few differences. In Battlefield 4, we've updated the Fighter Jet class to Stealth Jets … and their customization options now focus on air-to-air fighting. Attack Jets now have their own set of unlocks which focus on air-to-ground capabilities." [verified] — battlelog.battlefield.com/bf3/news/view/the-road-to-battlefield-4-vehicles/

Rule R8 is BF2042's Hovercraft. From the EA launch briefing: "We designed this vehicle to behave as an alternative to the LATV4, but not as an upgrade. In Thursday's Update #2, we have balanced the health of this vehicle to bring it more in line with its design." [verified] — ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-2042/news/battlefield-briefing-launch-update-and-the-road-ahead

Readability ceiling. ~6 chassis (BF2142) and ~12 classes (BF4) is roughly what a HUD and a roster can absorb; BFH's 4 is the comfortable floor. WoT shows the opposite extreme — hundreds of vehicles — which works only because class (5 types) is the HUD unit and the individual tank is just stats. Our 7 chassis × 20 variants sits deliberately in the BF1-to-BF4 band, not in WoT's.
