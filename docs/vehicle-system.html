Vehicle System — chassis, variants, and the honor bracket

Companion to GAME-DESIGN.md and systems/class-and-weapon-families.md. Implements the vehicle half of pillar P1 (weapon choice is the game) applied to vehicles, and answers Omar's two questions:

"Leveling can also be power, not only choice. Because that way we can have different quests and work for different levels. Also restricting which levels can fight against which levels (we can call it 'honor'). That's for the future though. Don't worry about it."

"Also think about the different vehicles there are in BFH. Could we have a few more? Maybe different specialized types for the different ones there are already?"

Research basis: research/05-vehicle-variant-precedent.md, research/bfh_map_vehicles.json, research/02-bfh-system-inventory.md. All primary sources fetched live on 2026-11-08.

0. Scope and status

Part Status
Vehicle chassis × variant system (§2–§5) Designed here. Ready for implementation.
Honor brackets / level-gated PvP (§6) Deferred by Omar (2026-11-08). Spec sketched so it is not lost; not built.

Omar's framing of leveling-as-power is kept as a live option, because it changes the progression architecture and is expensive to retrofit. §6 records exactly what it would require. It is deliberately not implemented now.

Two lessons, both load-bearing for our design:

  1. BFH gated vehicles per map. Infantry-only maps had zero vehicles. That is a deliberate, load-bearing map property, not a content accident — 5 of 14 maps had no vehicles at all.
  2. The Jeep is the universal chassis. It appears on 6 maps; every other chassis appears on at most 5. If we add variants, the Jeep chassis is where they will be read most often — and where a bad variant will be felt most.

1.3 The original's weakness

BFH's vehicles were chassis without roles. A jeep is a jeep; a tank is a tank. There is no decision at the spawn screen beyond "do I want wheels". Compare Battlefield 1, where one chassis is three different vehicles depending on loadout — and this is exactly the shape of the thing Omar is asking for.

2. The system: chassis × variant

A chassis is a hull. A variant is a role. The chassis decides physics, silhouette and seat layout; the variant decides the weapon mount, the gadget, the armour profile and the spawn cost.

The design rule, borrowed from BF1 and BF2042:

Every variant must be the best vehicle at its job and the worst vehicle at every other job. If a variant is merely "a bit better" at something, it is a stat change, not a new vehicle.

2.1 Why BF1 is the precedent, not BF2

[verified] — gameranx.com/features/id/52014/article/battlefield-1-vehicles-2016/, battlefield1-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicle, gamespot.com/gallery/all-of-battlefield-1s-vehicles/2900-940/.

BF1 shipped four chassis, three variants each, twelve vehicles, and every variant was a different vehicle in practice:

Chassis Variants
FT-17 Light Tank Close Support (medkits, poison gas, deployable) · Flanker (fast, 20mm auto-cannon, mine-laying) · Howitzer (75mm, smokescreen, emergency repair)
A7V Heavy Tank Heavy Assault (57mm + 5 MGs, smokescreen) · Breakthrough (57mm + 20mm, medkit, emergency repair) · Heavy Flamethrower (gas cloud, flamethrowers)
Mark V Landship Squad Support (5 seats, repair + ammo + medkit crates) · Tank Hunter (13.2mm anti-tank) · Mortar (rear mortar, smoke/gas shells)
Artillery Truck Armored Mortar Truck · Armored Anti-Aircraft Truck · Armored Artillery Truck

Note the pattern: the variants change the seat count, not just the gun. A Landship Squad Support carries five; a Tank Hunter carries three. A variant is a different vehicle that happens to share a chassis, exactly as we want.

BF1 also gated by package: you owned a class (tank/truck/landship) and unlocked its variants by playing. That is our progression hook, and it maps directly onto the class spec system already designed in systems/class-and-weapon-families.md.

2.2 Why BF2042 is the cautionary tale

[verified] — ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-2042/news/dev-notes-state-of-vehicle-gameplay (EA dev notes, 30 Nov 2023). Two named failure modes:

"Frequent feedback we've heard from the Community was around the prevalence of explosive weaponry, and unclear combat roles across vehicles."

The fix: "we fully overhauled all vehicle loadouts to ensure combat roles were once again clearly defined. This largely meant that some vehicles such as the LATV4 Recon which was meant as a transport vehicle, no longer had access to explosive weaponry."

Also, from the same notes, on the EMKV90-TOR:

"We felt reducing its long-range combat potency was necessary, but to compensate, we also increased its close combat power to allow for a more aggressive role."

And on jets:

"In previous Battlefield titles, jets could dominate an entire match by themselves. We didn't want that for Battlefield 2042 and changed their role to focus on air combat, while making ground support capabilities secondary."

Three rules we take directly from this:

  1. A transport chassis carries no explosives. No launcher, no AT rocket, no mine. The Recon/APC/Jeep transport may not kill vehicles. Full stop.
  2. Every variant has one battlefield line, not one "strongest at everything". The TOR was nerfed at range and buffed at close range, not buffed everywhere. A variant that is good everywhere is a variant that is bad at being interesting.
  3. Air chassis do not ground-clear. A vehicle that can kill tanks, planes and infantry from a safe position deletes the infantry's reason to exist.

2.3 The one rule that matters most for NEUTRALIA

BF2042's problem was unclear combat roles. Our answer is structural, not numeric:

A vehicle variant may not be chosen in the field. It is chosen at the spawn screen, and it is one of three. Each spawn offers one variant per chassis, never more. The player picks a role before the match and plays it. There is no "the tank spawned and I happen to be in it".

This is BF1's model. It converts a vehicle from a lottery into a decision.

3.2 Totals

7 player-selectable chassis · 20 variants, plus the map-only emplacement (C8) which players never choose. Arithmetic: 3+3+3+3+3+3+2 = 20.

Readability test: BFH had 6 chassis and 0 variants; we have 7 chassis and 20 variants. The variant count is what makes each match's spawn screen a decision. The chassis count stays low enough that a player can hold all of it in their head.

4. How a variant is earned

Reuses the class-spec model from systems/class-and-weapon-families.md — no new economy.

Layer What it does Cost
Chassis Unlocked by level / faction standing. 8 chassis total. Level gates
Variant Unlocked by using that chassis. 3 per chassis. Chassis-specific XP, like Weapon Skill Points
Loadout Chosen at spawn, free, changeable every match. Nothing

The middle layer is the important one and it is the same trick as the weapon tree: variants are earned by playing the chassis, not by levelling. A player who owns six chassis and spreads their time masters one or two; a player who commits has all three variants of one chassis. Same math, same lesson.

Every match, the spawn screen offers exactly one variant per chassis you have unlocked. You may not carry two variants of the same chassis, and you may not drive a variant you have not unlocked. The spawn screen shows, per chassis, the three variant icons with the unlocked one lit.

Cost to the match: one chassis slot. A player who wants the Heavy Tank cannot also take the Fighter. This is the decision.

5. Balancing rules

5.1 Hard rules

Rule Source
R1. A transport chassis carries no explosive weapon. BF2042 LATV4 Recon rework (EA dev notes)
R2. Every variant has exactly one battlefield line it dominates. BF2042 TOR: range cut, close buffed
R3. Air chassis do not ground-clear. BF2042 jets: air role primary, ground secondary
R4. A variant never changes the chassis silhouette or seat count upward. BF1 variants do change seat count (downward) — we follow BF1: a variant may reduce seats, never add them
R5. No variant may be selected in the field. It is a spawn-screen choice. §2.3
R6. Anti-vehicle capability belongs to exactly one chassis per domain. BF2 role separation (MBT / AA / APC each distinct)
R7. A chassis that appears on many maps must have the lowest power ceiling. §1.2 — the Jeep is on 6 maps; a strong Jeep is a strong Jeep on every map
R8. A variant must be worse than the chassis baseline at something. BF2042 Hovercraft: designed "as an alternative to the LATV4, but not as an upgrade" — players used it as a strict upgrade, so EA cut its health in Update #2 (EA launch briefing). A variant with no downside is an upgrade.
R9. Two variants that cannot be told apart in one sentence are the same variant. DICE on BF3: Fighter Jets and Attack Jets "were both practically the same class, with few differences" — fixed in BF4 by splitting them into Stealth Jets (air-to-air) and Attack Jets (air-to-ground) with separate unlock trees (BF4 Battlelog).

R8 and R9 came out of the precedent research after the first design pass and are the two rules that prevent the failure BF2042 and BF4 both had to fix in public.

R8 exists because BF2042 shipped a Hovercraft that was meant to be an alternative to the LATV4, and the community read it as a straight upgrade. EA's own words, from the launch briefing: "We designed this vehicle to behave as an alternative to the LATV4, but not as an upgrade." They had to cut its health in Update #2 to put it back on its intended line. The rule is now enforced at design time: every variant must be worse than its chassis at something.

R9 exists because DICE said out loud that BF3's two jet classes "were both practically the same class, with few differences" — and fixed it in BF4 by splitting them into Stealth Jets (air-to-air) and Attack Jets (air-to-ground) with separate unlock trees. The lesson generalises: if you cannot describe two variants in one differing sentence, they are one variant with a different paint job.

What R8 looks like in practice, in our roster:

Chassis Variant Worse than the chassis at…
Light tank Javelin armour (light hull)
Light tank Hunter everything but anti-vehicle
Heavy tank Siege direct fire (arc trajectory)
Fighter Bomber dogfighting (no cannon)
Fighter Liaison speed and survivability
Helicopter Spotter everything — it carries no weapon
Boat Gunboat amphibious use (water-bound)

Threat Countered by
Light tank Fighter Bomber (bombs vs armour), AT infantry
Heavy tank Scout car (never engage — outrun it), Heavy Tank Hunter, air
Air (fighter) Emplacement AA, Heavy Tank AA? no — AA emplacement only, plus Flak infantry
Air (helicopter) Light Tank Hunter, AA emplacement, AT infantry
Infantry Fighter Bomber, Gunboat, Gunship
Scout car (unarmoured transport) Fighter Bomber — the scout has no armour to stop bombs
Player in a vehicle Infantry AT weapons, sticky TNT (BFH-verified: sticks to vehicles)

Sticky TNT sticking to vehicles is [verified] in the original (battlefield.fandom.com/wiki/Battlefield_Heroes: "Sticky TNT/ Sticky TNT - Short range/ Medium radius (Standard for all classes, sticks to walls and vehicles)"). It is a free gift for the vehicle game: infantry can always threaten vehicles.

5.3 Spawn economy

  • A vehicle spawns only at a controlled point, like BFH. [verified] — BFH vehicles spawned when you arrived at the point, no waiting.
  • One vehicle per point per chassis. A point offering Heavy Tank does not also offer the Light Tank. This is the scarcity that makes the chassis choice matter.
  • A point's chassis is map-defined. Infantry-only maps have no points. BFH had 5 of 14 maps with no vehicles; we keep that discipline.

6. Honor — level-gated PvP (DEFERRED)

Omar's direction, parked on 2026-11-08: "That's for the future though. Don't worry about it." Recorded here so the design consequence is captured before it becomes expensive.

6.1 What Omar proposed

"Leveling can also be power, not only choice. Because that way we can have different quests and work for different levels. Also restricting which levels can fight against which levels (we can call it 'honor')."

Two independent ideas, and they are separable:

  • (a) Level = vertical power. Contradicts pillar P2 ("Leveling unlocks options, never power"). Accepting it means level 30 vs level 1 is a real gap, which enables level-gated content and level-gated PvP.
  • (b) Honor = the bracket system. Restricts which levels may fight which levels, and can carry the PvP rank ladder.

6.2 Precedent that exists

[verified] — vanilla.splashgame.org/pvp-honor-system.php, wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Honor_Points, ubisoft.com/.../ranked-30-update, esportsinsider.com/rainbow-six-siege-ranks.

Game System Notes
WoW (vanilla) Honor ranks 1–14 with battleground level brackets 10–19 / 20–29 / 30–39 / 40–49 / 50–59 / 60 Rank-gated rewards. Rank 7+ had real PvP gear. Also the cautionary tale: "it will be nothing but cruel" — free-for-all honour grinding in the open world punished low-level players.
WoW Cataclysm Honor (low tier, capped) + Conquest (high tier, weekly cap), removed in Legion Two-tier currency split.
R6 Siege Skill (hidden) vs Rank (visible RP ladder); squad restrictions — Copper–Emerald may queue within 3 ranks, Diamond–Champion within 2 Ranked 3.0 removed hidden MMR so "your skill is truly your rank".

6.3 The design consequence we must accept

If level carries power, then level brackets are not optional — they are mandatory. Without them a level 30 with better gear deletes a level 10 in open world, and that is the WoW complaint quoted above. So (a) forces (b).

6.4 Sketch (to be designed properly when the time comes)

  • Brackets by level band, not by a single number. 1–10, 11–20, 21–30, plus an open band.
  • Honor = the PvP rank carried on top of level. Separate currency from level XP.
  • A bracket's queue mixes only that bracket. Different bands do not meet.
  • Open-world PvP is either disabled across brackets or honour-gated — this is the WoW lesson, and the reason the original honour system is worth studying rather than copying.
  • Rewards bracket-specific. A rank that is unbeatable at level 30 is worthless if the level 10 bracket cannot see it.
  • The Squad Support / Spotter / Recovery variants (§3) are the honest picks for bracket play — they reward coordination over aim.

6.5 Why the deferral is cheap

Nothing in the vehicle system depends on honor. The spawn-screen variant choice, the chassis economy and the vehicle loadouts all work at a single level band. If honor is designed later, it slots in as a queue rule and a reward track — it does not require rebuilding vehicles.

7. What is deliberately not designed yet

  • Vehicle physics. Handling model, armour model, hit-point values. The original used a simple HP number per vehicle (Jeep 120, Tank 180, Plane 190, AA 250) and we keep the shape of that — one HP pool plus role-specific armour multipliers — but the numbers are not set here.
  • Seat-by-seat weapon mounts. BF1's exact mount table (which seat has which gun) is the model to copy; the specific table is implementation.
  • Vehicle class/spec tree, in the style of systems/class-and-weapon-families.md. The variant unlock curve (how much chassis XP per variant) is not set.
  • Exact map vehicle assignments. §1.2 gives the BFH baseline; our maps are not designed.

8. Verification checklist for implementation

When this system is built, assert mechanically:

  1. Every chassis has 2–3 variants (plus the emplacement).
  2. No two variants of the same chassis share a loadout.
  3. No transport variant carries an explosive (R1).
  4. Each variant has exactly one dominant battlefield line, and no line is claimed by two variants of the same chassis (R2).
  5. Every chassis has a counter and a prey (rock-paper-scissors is closed).
  6. Every chassis appears on at least one map and on no more than 8 maps (R7).
  7. A variant is never selectable in the field — only at spawn (R5).
  8. The spawn screen shows at most one variant per chassis, and at most one chassis of each domain at a time.

Done: tools/simulate-design.py now carries sim_vehicle_matrix(), asserting V1–V6 plus the seat-economy and transport-share checks. It runs as part of the normal ALL CHECKS PASSED gate.

The simulator found two real design flaws on its first run, which were fixed in the design rather than papered over in the assertion: - Contested role lines. The Carrier and Recovery both claimed SUPPORT, and Bulwark and Javelin both fought anti-vehicle. Now every variant of a chassis owns a distinct battlefield line (Armoured car: TRANSPORT / ANTI_INF / SUPPORT · Light tank: ANTI_INF / HEAVY / ANTI_VEH · Heavy tank: HEAVY / HOLD / INDIRECT). - The Scout car had no predator. A transport that nothing can hunt is a safe ride. The Fighter Bomber now counts as its counter — unarmoured transports are what bombs are for.

Both are now enforced: changing the matrix to reintroduce either flaw fails the build.
