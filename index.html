The core idea

A class is not a stat block. A class is the set of weapon families it may use, plus one utility only it can perform. The 2009 original already had this backbone — seven of its nine weapon families were class-locked — but the Soldier held six of the nine and 97 of 185 weapons, so one class was a superset of the game.

This is Orthogonal Differentiation: differences that are in type rather than in value (GDP3, Chalmers University of Technology; coined by Harvey Smith, 2003).

Tooling & research

Progression simulator — tools/simulate-design.py (pure stdlib). Validates the level curve against real scraped BFH numbers, the weapon-tree point economy, Doctrine capacity, the class-matrix invariants, and measures the original roster's stat-clone ratios from source data on every run.

DOM tests — prototype/test-prototype.js (39 checks) and prototype/test-classes.js (44 checks) drive the pages in headless Chromium via CDP and assert real DOM state, not what the markup claims.

Research corpus — research/ holds the scraped primary sources (weapon roster, item detail, abilities, maps, level curve) plus four written research reports.

Status

Design phase. The original game's systems were measured from primary sources (the EA wiki, bfh-info, and the scraped item database); every factual claim in the documents is marked [verified] or traced to a cited file. Several earlier claims were wrong and have been corrected in-place with the retraction noted — the corrections are part of the record, not hidden.
